# Customer-Segmentation-and-Sales-Insight-Using-EDA

This project applies Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) techniques to uncover meaningful insights from customer and sales data. The goal is to identify distinct customer segments, analyze purchasing behavior, and provide actionable recommendations that can improve marketing strategies, product targeting, and overall business performance.

Features
- Data Cleaning & Preprocessing: Handling missing values, outliers, and formatting inconsistencies.
- Exploratory Analysis: Statistical summaries, distributions, and correlation checks.
- Customer Segmentation: Grouping customers based on demographics, purchase frequency, and spending patterns.
- Sales Insights: Identifying top products, seasonal trends, and high‑value customers.
- Visualization: Interactive charts and plots to highlight key findings.

 Outcomes & Insights
- Clear segmentation of customers into meaningful groups (e.g., high spenders, frequent buyers, occasional customers).
- Identification of products and categories driving the majority of sales.
- Seasonal and regional sales trends for better inventory and marketing planning.
- Actionable recommendations for targeted campaigns and customer retention.
